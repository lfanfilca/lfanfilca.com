FAQ

Quick answers

The things people ask us most often. Don't see your question? Drop us a line.

Where is L FanfilCA based?

We are a globally distributed studio with team members across Europe, North America and Asia. Our apps are designed in Europe, tested worldwide, and published under the L FanfilCA brand on Google Play and the App Store.

Do you take on external client work?

Yes — selectively. We partner with brands that share our values around privacy, age-appropriate design and ethical monetisation. See Services or get in touch.

How do you handle children's privacy?

All of our apps that may appeal to children are designed to be compliant with COPPA, GDPR-K, the Google Play Families Policy and the Apple Kids category. We do not serve personalised ads to known child users.

How can I delete my account or data?

Open the in-app Settings → Privacy → Delete account, or email us at spencerk@ho-weicar.pics. We will action your request within 30 days.

Where can I read your legal documents?

Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are linked in the footer of every page. Both are written in plain English and cover every market we operate in.

Ready to build something delightful?

Whether you have a polished product spec or just a napkin sketch, we love hearing from teams that want to make mobile better. Say hi 👋
