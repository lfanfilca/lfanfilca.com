How we work

From napkin sketch to global launch.

Our studio process is built on transparency, small iterations and weekly demos. Here's how a typical engagement runs.

1 · Discover

Two-week sprint: market scan, user interviews, prototype, and a go / no-go on the problem worth solving.

2 · Design

Brand, motion system, art direction and an interactive clickable prototype validated with real users.

3 · Build

Native Android (Kotlin) and iOS (Swift) with shared design system. Weekly demos, public beta at 70%.

4 · Launch

Store submission, ASO, store-listing creative, press kit and a 30-day post-launch live-ops plan.

5 · Care

Live-ops, content drops, OS upgrades, ad mediation tuning, store policy compliance. We stay for the long haul.

6 · Localise & scale

Translation, in-app cultural review, store listing localisation, regional ad networks and pricing.
